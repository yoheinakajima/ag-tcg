Part D — Lifecycle policy spec
Create docs/TOURNAMENT_CANDIDATE_LIFECYCLE.md.

Define statuses exactly:
- active
- held_probe
- family_champion
- portfolio_anchor
- probation
- retired
- quarantined
- special_pilot_only
- invalid

Define protected statuses:
- held_probe
- family_champion
- portfolio_anchor
- special_pilot_only
- any candidate explicitly marked live_control / water_family_current_best / manual_hold
- immediate parent of an active child, if lineage data exists

Define never-schedule statuses:
- retired
- quarantined
- special_pilot_only
- invalid

Define lifecycle rules v0:
- New or under-sampled schedulable candidates remain probation until they meet placement sample requirements.
- Any candidate with invalid/timeout/error rate above threshold becomes quarantined.
- A candidate with zero games remains probation unless protected.
- A candidate with enough games and no hard failures may become active.
- Per family, the strongest sufficiently sampled candidate may become family_champion, but only if its Wilson lower bound and head-to-head evidence justify it.
- A candidate can be retired only by status mark, never by tarball deletion.
- Never retire held_probe, portfolio_anchor, special_pilot_only, or family_champion unless an explicit manual override exists.
- No promotion to Kaggle is possible in this pass.
- No “strictly better” claim unless Wilson intervals and parent/child/self-mirror controls support it. Otherwise label as no_regression / inconclusive / needs_more_games.
