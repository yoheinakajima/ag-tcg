Part F — Core competency fixtures

Create fixture directory:

data/fixtures/core_competency/

Create JSON fixtures and an index:

data/fixtures/core_competency/index.yaml

Fixtures should be deterministic observations or reduced observation-like structures. They do not need to be full game replays if a reduced structure can test the decision logic.

Create at least these fixtures:

1. setup_active_prefers_strong_basic_attacker
Given Kyogre and Snover as legal setup-active options:
Expected: choose Kyogre unless playbook says fast evolution setup dominates.

2. setup_bench_adds_backup
Given active Kyogre and legal bench Kyogre/Snover:
Expected: bench at least one useful backup/setup Pokémon when bench has space.

3. setup_bench_not_empty_if_active_fragile
Given active Snover and benchable Kyogre/Snover:
Expected: bench a backup.

4. attach_energy_to_active_attacker
Given active Kyogre and Water Energy attach options:
Expected: attach to active Kyogre.

5. attach_tool_to_attacker
Given Powerglass and active Kyogre:
Expected: attach tool to Kyogre rather than random bench.

6. evolve_when_line_ready
Given Snover in play and Mega Abomasnow ex legal:
Expected: choose evolution unless immediate attack/KO dominates.

7. ultra_ball_discards_excess_energy
Given Ultra Ball discard prompt with excess Water and setup pieces:
Expected: discard Water Energy before only Snover/Kyogre/Mega.

8. search_missing_basic_before_orphan_evolution
Given no Snover in play and ToHand choices include Snover and Mega Abomasnow:
Expected: choose Snover over orphan Mega.

9. search_evolution_when_basic_exists
Given Snover in play and ToHand choices include Mega Abomasnow:
Expected: choose Mega Abomasnow.

10. avoid_secret_box_without_safe_discards
Given main action includes Secret Box but fewer than 3 safe discards:
Expected: avoid Secret Box if productive alternative exists.
If the main option cannot be resolved to Secret Box deterministically, mark fixture advisory/ungradeable with explanation.

11. deckout_guard_low_deck
Given deckCount <= 4 and optional draw/search vs non-draw action:
Expected: avoid optional draw/search unless immediate win/survival.

12. attack_over_passive_draw_when_ready
Given attacker online and legal attack plus optional draw/search:
Expected: attack or attach/attack path, not passive draw/search.

13. promote_best_after_ko
Given choose-new-active context and bench has stronger attacker:
Expected: promote best attacker, not weakest card.

14. no_bench_loss_prevention
Given active near KO and bench option available:
Expected: develop backup before optional draw.

For each fixture include:
- id
- description
- context
- playbook
- expected behavior
- hard/advisory
- source: synthetic or replay-derived
- notes
